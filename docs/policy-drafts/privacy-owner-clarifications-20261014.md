## Meetings and AI assisted services

We use Zoom for client video calls. Zoom may generate recordings or meeting summaries when those features are enabled. We use Krisp for meeting transcription and potentially AI-generated notes and summaries. We have used Google Drive to store or share client meeting recordings and supporting resources. Recording, transcription and summarization depend on the meeting and enabled features; not every meeting is recorded. These activities can involve meeting audio, video where recording is enabled, transcripts, participant information and materials discussed or shared for the engagement.

Recording and transcription must be disclosed to participants and handled subject to applicable consent requirements. The specific notification and consent procedure is still being confirmed; we do not claim that a standardized process has already been verified. This policy is not blanket consent to recording or transcription.

Internal AI-assisted service delivery is distinct from public marketing use. We intend to use relevant meeting transcripts and client-approved information with AI tools to support summaries, strategic analysis, recommendations and deliverables within the agreed engagement. Use is limited to relevant service purposes and subject to appropriate confidentiality safeguards; unnecessary sensitive information should not be submitted. Zoom and Krisp are identified above. Any additional AI tools, the approved inputs, provider data-use and training settings, access and storage arrangements must be identified and reviewed before use; this notice does not authorize unspecified processing or promise that all providers exclude model training.

Files may be stored in the relevant cloud services, including Google Drive where used. The precise storage accounts and locations, access and sharing permissions, deletion and retention settings, and any use of submitted content for provider model training remain to be confirmed. We do not claim that content is never used for training or deleted on a fixed schedule. Please ask us about the handling before sharing sensitive information in a session.

## Optional educational and marketing content

We may ask whether you would like to participate in an educational video, case study or promotional piece based on a Strategy Sprint discussion or another engagement. This is a proposed optional use, not a statement that your materials are already used publicly. Internal recording, transcription or AI-assisted service delivery does not authorize public use.

Before publishing identifiable client recordings, clips, quotes, screenshots, names, logos, confidential business information or other client materials, we will obtain separate, optional, express authorization. We will identify the specific final materials, permitted channels, purpose and any agreed limits for your approval before publication. Approval of one item does not authorize other materials, edits that change their meaning, additional channels or future publications outside that approval.

You may decline marketing use entirely, consider anonymized content only, or consider identified content. For client-derived anonymized content, we will also request approval of the specific proposed materials before publication. Removing a name alone may not prevent identification; recognizable voices, images, distinctive facts or confidential details must be addressed. Material that cannot reliably be anonymized will be treated as identifiable and will need the corresponding express approval.

Marketing permission is not a condition of buying or receiving the Strategy Sprint, Audit, consultations or another service. Accepting or acknowledging this Privacy Policy, accepting payment terms, attending a meeting, approving recording or transcription, or approving internal AI assistance does not grant permission to publish client content. Silence, an unchecked option or a failure to respond is not marketing authorization.

You can contact Juda.Borrayo@gmail.com to decline or withdraw optional permissions. We will stop new uses covered by the withdrawal and arrange removal from channels we control where reasonably possible. We cannot guarantee retrieval of copies already saved or shared by others. Specific approval and withdrawal procedures remain to be finalized; no standard release process is represented as already implemented.

The separate draft form provides independent internal-use choices and a final-material approval record. It is not part of mandatory payment terms.
