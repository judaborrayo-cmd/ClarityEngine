# Privacy Policy refinements for final review

October 14, 2026 · Final public text approved for production publication. See the final deployment report for live verification.

The complete client-facing text is now in [Privacy Policy proposed final](privacy-policy-proposed-final.md), generated from shared/privacy-policy.ts. It covers all services, distinguishes current Zoom/Krisp/Drive practices from intended ChatGPT-assisted strategic work, preserves rule-based Assessment generation, reflects the observed website technologies, and keeps internal AI delivery separate from optional public marketing.

The proposed [routine meeting-consent process](routine-meeting-consent.md) uses one short pre-engagement notice and three independent Yes/No replies, with brief confirmations at meetings. It does not require a long release for each session or add anything to checkout. The [detailed release](client-recording-content-release.md) remains an optional internal template, including specific final-material marketing approval.

Public text no longer contains the internal configuration/readiness checklist. It retains material customer disclosures, including the absence of a standard retention period and site-wide cookie controls, without inventing locations, provider training rules or deletion promises. Account/storage/access/retention decisions and the Calendly banner correction are in [internal operational decisions](privacy-operational-decisions.md).

No GA4, GTM, Google Ads or Meta technology is added. No email-marketing provider is selected or represented as active. Only the finalized public policy is authorized for production publication. Consent documents remain separate; technical SEO and AI-search readiness remain the next priority and have not started.
