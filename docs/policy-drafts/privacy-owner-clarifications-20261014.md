## Meetings and AI assisted services

We use Zoom for client video calls. Zoom may generate recordings or meeting summaries when those features are enabled. We use Krisp for meeting transcription and potentially AI-generated notes and summaries. We have used Google Drive to store or share client meeting recordings and supporting resources. Recording, transcription and summarization depend on the meeting and enabled features; not every meeting is recorded. These activities can involve meeting audio, video where recording is enabled, transcripts, participant information and materials discussed or shared for the engagement.

Recording and transcription must be disclosed to participants and handled subject to applicable consent requirements. The specific notification and consent procedure is still being confirmed; we do not claim that a standardized process has already been verified. This policy is not blanket consent to recording or transcription.

When AI tools are used to help prepare summaries, recommendations or strategic deliverables, relevant meeting transcripts or client materials may be processed for those agreed service purposes, subject to appropriate confidentiality safeguards. Processing should be limited to relevant material; unnecessary sensitive information should not be submitted. Zoom and Krisp are identified above; any additional AI providers, the materials submitted, their account settings and confidentiality arrangements require confirmation before they are represented as established practices.

Files may be stored in the relevant cloud services, including Google Drive where used. The precise storage accounts and locations, access and sharing permissions, deletion and retention settings, and any use of submitted content for provider model training remain to be confirmed. We do not claim that content is never used for training or deleted on a fixed schedule. Please ask us about the handling before sharing sensitive information in a session.

## Live tracking findings

A live browser review did not confirm a Clarity Engine GA4 property, Google Tag Manager container, Google Ads conversion or remarketing tag, or Meta Pixel sending events for this website. The embedded Calendly booking page did load Google and Meta libraries within its own frame, and YouTube embeds made Google advertising-related requests. These provider activities do not establish that Clarity Engine has configured its own analytics or advertising accounts. Server-side advertising integrations and any off-site sale or advertising-sharing practices remain unverified.

Calendly and YouTube embeds, and Stripe checkout on its own pages, can use their own cookies or storage. Live browsing observed cookies associated with Calendly, YouTube, Stripe through the booking embed, and Replit. Google-hosted fonts and external scripts also cause requests to those providers. Their notices explain their handling of technical data. Calendly loads its own consent tooling in some booking paths; the booking fallback is configured to hide its banner. No site-wide cookie preference panel was observed. The consent arrangement and controls required for the locations we serve remain to be confirmed.

## Retention

Clarity Engine has not established a standard retention period. Fixed periods for inquiries, assessment records and analytics, onboarding documents, recordings or AI summaries, and financial records therefore are not stated here. Provider logs, backups and account records may have separate retention rules. No deletion deadline or training exclusion is promised without verification.
