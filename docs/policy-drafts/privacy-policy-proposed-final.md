Client transcripts and approved materials may also support the internal AI-assisted workflow described below. That purpose is separate from public marketing. ## 7. Website technologies, cookies and choices

The Assessment uses first-party events for sales-page views, checkout starts, progress, question activity, report views and downloads, and work-together clicks. Events can include a session identifier, question identifier and timestamp. Session-associated events are not necessarily anonymous. The API also uses a hashed IP-derived identifier for request rate limiting; hashing is not a guarantee of anonymity.

Assessment purchase verification uses a first-party cookie named clarity_audit_purchase with a configured lifetime of 30 days. It is restricted to /api/audit, HttpOnly and SameSite=Lax, and Secure when configured for HTTPS. Blocking or clearing it before verification may affect access in the purchasing browser. Its expiration does not delete the associated records.

The homepage waitlist modal uses local storage named ce_waitlist_modal_suppressed_until to remember dismissal or submission for seven days. The entry contains a suppression date, not the form's name or email, and can remain until you clear site storage.

Calendly booking embeds and YouTube videos load external resources and can use provider cookies. Live browsing observed Calendly, YouTube, Replit and Stripe cookies, including Stripe resources loaded through Calendly. Google-hosted fonts and external Calendly/Replit scripts also cause requests to their providers. The Calendly booking frame loaded Google and Meta libraries, and YouTube frames made Google advertising-related requests; these are provider activities, not confirmation of Clarity Engine's own advertising tracking.

The live review did not confirm a first-party Clarity Engine GA4, Google Tag Manager, Google Ads or Meta Pixel installation. Server-side advertising integrations have not been verified. No new advertising tracking is described as active in this policy.

No site-wide cookie-preference control was observed. Calendly provides controls for its own cookies, whose availability depends on the embed and provider configuration. Provider controls do not manage all website technologies. You can manage cookies and site storage through your browser and provider privacy controls; blocking resources can affect booking, videos or checkout.

## 9. Confidentiality and protection

Confidential client materials are for the agreed service purposes; public use requires the separate authorization described above. Appropriate provider handling and access arrangements are needed for the particular information and engagement. Providers may process information in countries other than your own; no specific storage location, transfer mechanism or data-residency guarantee is stated here.

The public website uses HTTPS. Implemented Assessment safeguards include input validation, rate limiting, payment verification and hashed access tokens. Private report/resume responses include no-store, no-referrer and noindex directives. The Assessment API's diagnostics are designed to omit request bodies, cookies, tokens and private URLs; hosting and provider logs can be separate.

Anyone with a valid private Assessment or report link can access the associated information. Keep those links private and contact us if one is exposed. Downloaded or shared copies are outside the website's access controls. No system is completely secure; this policy does not guarantee absolute protection or independently verified security certifications.

## 10. Retention

Clarity Engine has not established a standard retention period across inquiries, client documents, recordings, transcripts, summaries, Assessment data or financial records. No fixed deletion deadline is promised.

Provider logs, backups and account records can have their own retention rules. Contact us to request a review of information held about you and deletion where applicable. Relevant service needs, legal recordkeeping obligations and applicable rights will be considered for the particular records, with any limitation explained when your request is handled.
