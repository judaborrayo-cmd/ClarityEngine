# Routine meeting consent process

Proposed operating process for Strategy Sprints, consultations and Audits. Not a checkout requirement; no claim that it is already implemented. The detailed Client Recording & Content Release remains an optional internal template for special circumstances and item-specific marketing approval.

## Before the first meeting

Send the short notice below with the current Privacy Policy and three independent Yes/No choices. A written email reply is sufficient as the routine record where applicable requirements permit; do not require a long release or signature for every meeting. Scope approval to the named engagement, enabled tools and approved input categories. Obtain each participant's choice; blank, ambiguous or conflicting replies are not permission.

Fill in the actual recording format, tools/account disclosure and client information proposed for further analysis before sending. Do not enable an unreviewed AI account or conceal unknown provider handling behind a general checkbox. If the necessary tool/privacy review is incomplete, leave that activity off and use manual notes or another appropriate alternative.

### Reusable notice and reply

> We use Zoom for our meetings and offer optional Krisp transcription and meeting summaries. With your separate approval, selected relevant transcript excerpts and client-approved information may also be used with an approved ChatGPT account for internal strategic analysis, recommendations and deliverables. This is not permission to publish your recording, quotes, name or other materials. You can decline optional activities or ask us to stop.
>
> Engagement/meetings covered: **[name and scope]**
> Recording proposed: **[audio only / audio and video; identify any screen capture]**
> Tools, data-handling notice and Privacy Policy: **[approved tools/account disclosure and notice link/version]**
> Information approved for further AI analysis: **[specific materials or categories agreed with client]**
>
> Please reply separately:
> 1. Optional retained Zoom recording as described above: **Yes / No**
> 2. Optional Krisp transcription and meeting summaries (and Zoom summaries only if disclosed): **Yes / No**
> 3. Further internal AI analysis of the approved information with the disclosed ChatGPT account/tool: **Yes / No**
>
> Marketing use is **not requested or authorized by these choices** and is never a condition of purchasing or receiving services. Any proposed public material requires a separate review and express approval of its final version, use and channels.

Transcription can process live audio even when no recording is retained. If the client approves further AI analysis but declines transcription, use only the separately approved existing documents/information. Client approval is not a substitute for reviewing provider confidentiality, training, retention or sharing settings.

## At each meeting

Briefly confirm the recorded choices: “For today, the agreed choices are [recording], [transcription/summaries] and [further internal AI]. Is that still correct? You can ask us to stop.” Do this before capture; log any changes without using an unapproved recording to capture consent. Apply required platform notices as well, and repeat the permission check for late joiners or changed tools, scope, recording format or inputs. Silence or attendance does not change a No into a Yes.

Keep a small consent record: engagement, participant, notice version, tool/input scope, three choices, date and evidence reference. Store the client reply rather than generating a new form per session. Record confirmations, objections and withdrawals, and stop the affected activity if permission is missing or withdrawn. Confirm local recording/transcription requirements before choosing whether oral confirmation is sufficient.

## Public content is a separate workflow

Do not seek marketing consent as part of this routine notice. For a particular clip, testimonial, case study or other client-derived item, show the exact final version, identification level (anonymized or identifiable), purpose, channels, period and restrictions. Obtain separate express written approval before publishing. Use section C of the optional detailed release or an equivalent item-specific approval email. Each identifiable participant/material owner must have the relevant authority or their own approval. Changing material meaning, identification or uses requires renewed approval.

## Before adopting this process

Finalize enabled features/account settings, notice/disclosure, storage/access, retention/deletion and the person handling withdrawals. Standard retention has not yet been established. This proposed process cannot establish that these operational settings or a jurisdiction-specific recording-consent procedure have already been verified.
