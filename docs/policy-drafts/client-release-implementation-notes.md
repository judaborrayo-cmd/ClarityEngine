# Client release implementation notes

This form and the expanded Privacy Policy are drafts. No meeting-consent or publication-approval workflow is claimed to be implemented, and nothing has been added to checkout or made a payment condition.

Before using the form, identify the actual enabled Zoom/Krisp features and any additional AI tools; provide the current notice plus account/storage/access, retention and provider-data-use information. Do not collect a signature on a blank or unexplained disclosure. No standard retention period has been established. Confirm regional recording/transcription requirements and operational settings; a platform recording notice alone is not verification of the full process.

Keep the signed internal choices separate from optional marketing preference and final-material approvals. Unselected/conflicting answers are not consent. Give each participant their own choice. Do not start capture until required permissions are obtained; handle late joiners and a request to stop. Arrange manual notes or another appropriate delivery method when capture/AI is declined.

For each marketing item, keep the approved final version, exact excerpt, identification level, purpose, channels, period, restrictions, benefit/compensation if any, signer authority and dated express approval. General permission to consider proposals does not permit publication. Obtain any other participant/material-owner approvals, apply redactions and assess re-identification risk before publication. Preserve quote meaning and verify facts/outcome claims; disclose material connections when required.

Document an approval and withdrawal contact/workflow, the person responsible, removal from controlled channels and limits on third-party copies. Do not promise fixed removal/deletion deadlines or excluded provider model training without verification. Recordings, transcripts and release records need an approved access and retention/deletion plan.

References informing the separate-purpose, affirmative-choice draft: [ICO valid consent guidance](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/lawful-basis/consent/what-is-valid-consent/), [Zoom recording-consent settings](https://support.zoom.com/hc/en/article?id=zm_kb&sysparm_article=KB0068402), and [FTC reviews/testimonials guidance](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/consumer-reviews-testimonials-rule-questions-answers). These are not a determination that one specific jurisdiction applies, or proof that platform/account settings already meet the requirements.
