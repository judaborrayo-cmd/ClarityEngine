# Client release implementation notes

The detailed Client Recording & Content Release is an optional internal template. Use [routine meeting consent](routine-meeting-consent.md) for normal Sprints, consultations and Audits. Neither document is a mandatory checkout requirement or automatic marketing release.

Routine process: disclose the enabled tools and purposes, collect three independent Yes/No replies, briefly reconfirm before capture, log changes/late joiners and respect withdrawals. Blank or unclear replies are not consent. Do not use an unapproved recording to capture consent; do not treat attendance as approval. Follow applicable participant requirements and platform notices.

Marketing is separate: show the specific final clip/quote/case study/material, purpose, identification level, channels, period and restrictions. Obtain express written approval of that item (section C of the detailed release or equivalent approval email). Obtain each relevant participant/material-owner authorization, check anonymity, preserve meaning and disclose material connections when required. A preference to consider proposals is not publication approval.

The actual provider/account settings, storage/access, retention and withdrawal ownership still need decisions before use. See [privacy operational decisions](privacy-operational-decisions.md). No standard retention period, excluded model training or implemented workflow is invented.

References: [ICO valid consent guidance](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/lawful-basis/consent/what-is-valid-consent/), [Zoom recording-consent settings](https://support.zoom.com/hc/en/article?id=zm_kb&sysparm_article=KB0068402), [FTC testimonial guidance](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/consumer-reviews-testimonials-rule-questions-answers). They do not establish applicable jurisdictions or verify current account settings.
