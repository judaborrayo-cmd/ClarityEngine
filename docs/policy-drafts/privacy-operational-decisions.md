# Privacy operational decisions and technical follow-up

Internal only · October 14, 2026. Companion to the proposed final public Privacy Policy, routine meeting-consent process and optional detailed release. This checklist is not public policy text and is not a checkout condition.

## Confirmed direction

The notice covers all services, including Assessment, Sprint, Audit, consultations and future engagements. Zoom is already used for meetings; Krisp transcription and potential summaries, optional Zoom recordings/summaries and past Google Drive storage/sharing were owner-confirmed. The intended routine is Zoom meetings → permitted Krisp transcription/summaries → separately approved transcript/client information in an approved AI tool such as ChatGPT for analysis and deliverables. Further ChatGPT processing is intended; no current client processing or automated website integration is asserted.

No email-marketing platform is connected or selected. No new advertising tracking is authorized. General privacy/payment acceptance never authorizes client publication. Detailed release remains optional; routine consent uses a short notice and separate replies. No standard retention period has been established.

## Decisions required before routine capture or further AI processing

| Decision | What must be settled |
| --- | --- |
| Provider accounts and AI settings | Choose the actual Zoom/Krisp features and ChatGPT plan/workspace; review confidentiality terms, training/feedback, history/retention, sharing, connected apps and any additional processor. Do not assume ChatGPT Business, Enterprise, consumer or API rules are interchangeable. No blanket no-training or zero-retention claim. |
| Inputs and service purpose | Identify approved transcript portions/material categories, redact unnecessary identifiers and sensitive information, and document the client scope. Additional tools/inputs require an updated disclosure and approval. Do not upload client materials to an unreviewed account. |
| Storage and access | Identify recordings/transcripts/summaries/deliverables stored in Zoom, Krisp and Drive, actual account ownership/regions, who can access/export/share, and applicable provider contracts or transfer arrangements. Check link permissions, backups and copies. No unsupported residency/encryption/access guarantee in the public notice. |
| Retention and deletion | Establish purpose-based rules for raw capture, transcripts, summaries, deliverables, consent/releases, inquiries, Assessment/events/rate-limit records, financial records and backups. Identify deletion responsibility and provider limitations; no invented periods. |
| Consent operation | Adopt the proposed notice/reply record and meeting-start confirmation; identify responsible person, late-joiner/withdrawal procedure, and applicable participant jurisdictions. Confirm what written/oral evidence is sufficient and enable required platform notices. The process is proposed, not already verified. |
| Publication and privacy requests | Assign a contact/owner for access, correction, deletion and withdrawal; retain specific final-material approval/version/channel records, signer authority and other participants' permissions; define removal on controlled channels and explain third-party-copy limits. |
| Wider business disclosures | Confirm customer locations, relevant processing grounds/required regional notices, additional business identity/address or representative details, CRM/contact recordkeeping, off-site sale/advertising sharing and database provider/region. Do not make unverified no-sale, transfer, child-policy or compliance claims. |

OpenAI's official documentation describes protections tied to the applicable workspace and says retention/access depend on configuration. It is not verification of the owner's selected account, nor an API retention policy applicable to all ChatGPT use. [ChatGPT workspace/cloud security](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/enterprise/chatgpt-work-cloud-security). The policy therefore names ChatGPT as intended use and avoids a product-wide training or retention promise.
