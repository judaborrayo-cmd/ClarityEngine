A Galapagos story since the 1930s

Some places have views.
This one has a story.

In the 1930s, Gus Angermeyer and his brothers arrived in the Galapagos in search of a different life. They built their home on the bay using lava rock and driftwood.

That pioneering history is still present today, from the Angermeyer Cave to the remnants of the Nixe, a vessel tied to the earliest days of tourism in the archipelago.

Where is Angermeyer Waterfront Inn located?

On the waterfront of Punta Estrada in Puerto Ayora, Santa Cruz Island, Galapagos, Ecuador. The inn overlooks the bay and has its own private dock.

How do I reach the hotel from Puerto Ayora?

Take a short water-taxi ride from Puerto Ayora's passenger pier to the hotel's dock. The waterfront location offers a peaceful setting close to town.

Can I see wildlife from the property?

Marine iguanas, sea lions, pelicans and other seabirds are regularly seen around the waterfront terrace and dock. Sightings are part of nature and cannot be guaranteed.

Does the hotel offer tours and Galapagos packages?

Yes. The hotel presents multi-day packages and can help coordinate experiences including island excursions, snorkeling and transfers. Explore the published options to see current inclusions.

How do I check availability or reserve a room?
